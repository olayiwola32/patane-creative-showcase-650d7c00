# QOA Creative Tech Website Redesign

## Goal
Elevate the existing QOA Creative Tech website into a polished, bespoke agency experience while preserving its identity, accurate project evidence, client testimonials, contact form, fixed-price services, and Paystack checkout.

## Work
1. **Shared visual foundation:** retain the current brand colors and logo; establish a consistent editorial style with stronger typography, spacing, image presentation, navigation, and footer across screen sizes.
2. **Homepage:** reshape the first screen around the supplied positioning and headline direction, then add platform expertise, varied service previews, agency differentiators, authentic portfolio highlights, a clear project process, and a closing contact action.
3. **Existing pages:** apply the same visual direction to Services, About, Portfolio, Testimonials, Pricing, and Contact without removing authentic content or changing payment/contact behavior. Keep project evidence and existing working links intact.
4. **Team:** the brief calls for five specialists, and the owner will provide their names, roles, and photos. Do not invent the missing three profiles; use verified existing team information only until the five-person details arrive.
5. **Site details:** align navigation and footer presentation and refresh page title/description and social metadata to reflect the full-service positioning.

## Technical approach
- Continue using the existing React pages, router, shared layout, Tailwind utilities, and semantic theme tokens; preserve the current color values.
- Reuse the existing portfolio, team, and brand assets; do not fabricate client work, platform certifications, or team members.
- Keep the contact form, WhatsApp contact, pricing amounts, checkout, and payment integrations operational and unchanged.
- Verify the preview at desktop and mobile widths and check the build and browser errors after implementation.
